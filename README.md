> **About this fork.** This is [yethihahtwe/token-tv](https://github.com/yethihahtwe/token-tv), a fork of
> [click6067-ship-it/token-tv](https://github.com/click6067-ship-it/token-tv). What's different:
> - **GeekMagic SmallTV-Ultra support** (firmware Ultra-V9), detected automatically. The original SD_PRO clock works as before.
> - **Both usage windows on every face.** Each card shows the fuller window and adds a second bar for the other one
>   (for example 5-hour under weekly), with its own reset time. With three services on screen there is no room,
>   so cards keep one bar (Retro moves the second window into its side box).
>
> The live demo and the face gallery belong to the original project and show its faces.

Your Claude and Codex usage limits on a ~$5 Wi-Fi desk clock. No firmware flashing.  
**Try it:** [live demo](https://token-tv.vercel.app) or `npx github:yethihahtwe/token-tv demo`, no clock or login needed.  
**Set it up:** give this repo to your AI and say *"Set up TokenTV for me."* It follows [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).  
**Make your own face:** no clock needed, start from the [Game Boy example](examples/gameboy) and ask your AI to change it. [Guide](docs/clock-faces.md) · [Share it](https://github.com/click6067-ship-it/token-tv/issues/new?template=share_a_face.md)
